# CA (Chartered Accountant) — Quick-Start Guide

As a **CA** you handle accounting, taxation, and GST compliance. You have **read-only** access to the
financial data plus the dedicated GST dashboard, returns filing, and reconciliation. You cannot approve
orders, run fulfilment, or change catalog/customer/order data.

## Where you land
After sign-in you go straight to **GST & Accounting**. Your 4 mobile bottom tabs are **GST · Reports ·
Finance · My Profile**.

## GST & Accounting dashboard (`/ca/gst`)
- **Money in / out** and a **GSTR-3B-style** summary with **manual ITC** entry and **net payable**.
- **Rate-wise / HSN-wise / state-wise** taxable breakup.
- An **Export** segment — outside-India orders are taxed at a flat **18% IGST** and shown separately.
- **Period presets:** This month, Last month, Indian FY quarters, This FY, Last FY, or custom.
- **Drill-down:** click any row to see the contributing orders (customer due vs COD pending).
- **Export** the report to **CSV or PDF**.

## GST Filing
Analytics & Reports → **GST Filing**: a returns workspace with a period calendar, **prepare / file /
reopen**, filing **snapshots**, and filing-aware export (GSTR-1 data).

## GST Reconciliation
Analytics & Reports → **GST Reconciliation**: five compared figures with drill-down so you can catch
mismatches before filing.

## The books (read-only)
- **Finance:** Expenses, Profit & Loss.
- **Reconciliation** and **Returns** (view).
- **Accounting (general ledger):** Chart of Accounts, Day Book, Trial Balance, Ledger Statement,
  Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, Cash Flow. You can **view** vouchers; posting/reversal is Admin/
  Accountant-only.
- **Reports:** full financial reports, Excel/PDF export.

## Good to know
- Prices are **GST-inclusive** by default (an admin can switch the mode in Settings). Invoices show
  per-line discount + GST% and a per-rate CGST/SGST/IGST breakup.
- **RTO** reverses a sale with a credit note in the current period; **Cancel** is excluded from outward
  supplies — so a post-invoice order should be RTO'd, never cancelled.
- ITC on the dashboard is **manual** (purchases/expenses don't yet capture GST automatically).

## Golden rules
- Reconcile before you file — use GST Reconciliation to catch mismatches.
- You are read-only on operations; raise anything that needs a change (e.g. a wrong GSTIN) with an admin.
