# Team Lead — Quick-Start Guide

As a **Team Lead** you oversee the salespeople assigned to you, can punch orders on their behalf, and
track your team's performance. Your view is **read-only oversight** of orders — you can't approve,
dispatch, or configure the system.

## Where you land
After sign-in you land on the **Dashboard**. Your 4 mobile bottom tabs are **Dashboard · Orders ·
Performance · My Profile**.

## What you can see
- **Your team's orders** — the orders your assigned salespeople punched (plus any you punch yourself).
  You can list, search, open, and download invoices.
- A **team-scoped dashboard** with order status, team highlights (top performer, best lead source,
  delivery success), and a link to full Team Performance.

> You see your team's orders because an admin assigns salespeople to you (Teams page). If you have no
> team yet, you'll only see your own orders — ask an admin to assign your team.

## Team Performance
Analytics & Reports → **Team Performance** (your main tool):
- Period controls (Today / Last 7 days / This month / Custom).
- Team KPIs: revenue, AOV, delivery success, lead conversion, COD outstanding, target progress.
- A **salesperson leaderboard** — click a person to drill into their individual 360 (orders, metrics).
- **Lead-source conversion** (which source converts best).
- A **My orders / Team orders** split so you can separate your own punching from the team's.

## You can also punch orders
Orders → **New Order**. The order is attributed to you and scoped back to your team view. (You don't
get the salesperson-only Leads/Products pages.)

## Messaging
Use **Call** / **WhatsApp** from order and customer rows. You can also manage the shared **WhatsApp
templates** (Account & Settings → WhatsApp templates).

## Golden rules
- You oversee, you don't approve — approvals are an Admin/Accountant job.
- Use Team Performance to spot a lagging salesperson and reach out directly.
