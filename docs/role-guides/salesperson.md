# Salesperson — Quick-Start Guide

As a **Salesperson** you take orders, manage leads, and look after your own customers. You see **only
your own** orders, leads, customers, and reports.

## Where you land
After sign-in you land on the **Dashboard** ("My day"). Your 4 mobile bottom tabs are **New Order ·
Orders · Customers · Products**.

> You sign in with your **mobile number** as the username.

## Your dashboard ("My day")
- Today's orders and revenue, your **monthly target** progress, and the amount **to collect**.
- A **task inbox**: customers due for a reorder + win-back customers (each with Call / WhatsApp).
- Your **lead pipeline** at a glance.

## Take a new order — the main job
Orders → **New Order** (guided, or toggle **Quick mode**):
1. **Customer:** enter the mobile. If they've ordered before, accept the **pre-fill**. Pick the
   destination (India → pincode auto-fills city/state; or Outside India) and the **lead source**.
2. **Items:** add products (use Quick-add best-sellers and the "frequently bought together" chips).
   Rates must stay within each product's min–MRP band.
3. **Payment:** collect at least **₹100 upfront** (full or partial — balance is pay-on-delivery) and
   **upload the payment screenshot(s)** (required when any amount is received).
4. **Review:** check totals/GST, add a note or discount if needed, and **Save**. The order goes to
   **Pending Admin Approval**.

Good to know:
- You **can't** make a second same-day order for the **same product** for the same customer (different
  items same day are fine).
- An unfinished order is saved as a **draft** you can resume.
- **Reorder** and **Convert from lead** open New Order pre-filled.
- You can **edit your own order while it's still Pending**.

## If an order is rejected
Open it (Orders), read the **reason**, choose **Fix & resubmit** — the same order (same code) goes back
for approval with its history intact. This also covers **Payment Rejected** orders.

## Leads
CRM → **Leads** and **Due follow-ups**:
- Capture leads, move them New → Contacted → Quoted → Won/Lost, set follow-up dates.
- **Convert** a won lead into an order.
- You'll be reminded of overdue follow-ups.

## Customers
CRM → **Customers** (only your own): open Customer 360 for history, risk, tags/notes, and **Reorder
last order**. Call or WhatsApp straight from the list.

## Products & reports
- **Products:** read-only — look up price, stock, and details.
- **Reports:** your own **sales / product / customer** reports (money/operations reports are not shown).
- **Leaderboard:** your this-month ranking + order streak.

## Golden rules
- Always collect **₹100+ upfront** and attach the **screenshot**, or the order won't save.
- Review and edit the **pre-filled** customer details before saving.
- Keep leads moving and follow-ups on time.
