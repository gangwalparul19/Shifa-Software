# Admin — Quick-Start Guide

As an **Admin** you have full access: approvals, configuration, fulfilment oversight, finance, GST,
analytics, and user management.

## Where you land
After sign-in you land on the **Dashboard**. Your 4 mobile bottom tabs are **Approvals · Orders ·
Products · Reports**.

## Your dashboard
- A **"needs attention"** strip: Approvals waiting, Exceptions, Awaiting handover — all clickable.
- A **channel overview** (Portal vs Shopify vs All) with a period filter.
- A **team-wise sales** widget and live activity/notifications.

## Your daily flow
1. **Approve new orders** — Orders → **Approval Queue**.
   - Review each order's details and payment screenshots.
   - **Approve** (sends it to fulfilment) or **Reject** with a reason category + note.
   - Use **bulk approve** with Quick-select-by-date; a preview shows how many will be skipped.
   - You'll get a live toast + chime when a new order needs approval.
2. **Watch the Exception Center** — Orders → **Exception Center**: approvals waiting, payment issues,
   failed deliveries, unsettled claims, and top insights, each with a direct action.
3. **Keep fulfilment moving** — check Packing and the QuikShip/Shopify sync when needed.

## Things only you can do
- **Settings:** company + **GST config** (GSTIN, state, inclusive/exclusive pricing), logo,
  **auto-approval** (enable + max amount), the **Shopify** on/off switch, delivery states.
- **Users:** create/edit staff, assign roles, activate/deactivate, reset passwords.
- **Profile approvals:** approve staff profile change requests.
- **Teams:** assign salespeople to a team lead. **Team-wise Sales** shows per-team rollups.
- **Order management:** edit any PENDING/APPROVED order, **Cancel Order** (even after an AWB — aborts
  the courier pickup), review **Deleted orders**.
- **Shopify Sync:** recover stuck Shopify orders to QuikShipX.
- **Audit Log** and **Backups**.
- **Announcements:** post banners all staff see.

## Order entry (you can also punch orders)
Orders → **New Order**. You can place an order **on behalf of** a specific salesperson/team lead.

## Finance & GST (you have full access)
Expenses, Profit & Loss, Reconciliation, the GST dashboard + filing + reconciliation, and the full
general ledger (Chart of Accounts, Voucher Entry, Day Book, Trial Balance, statements).

## Analytics
Analytics (targets/retention/forecast), Insights (recompute/dismiss), Leaderboard, Salespeople 360.

## Golden rules
- Use **RTO** (not Cancel) to give up on a post-invoice/delivered order — Cancel would drop a filed
  invoice from GST.
- Keep GST settings correct — invoices and the GST dashboard depend on your GSTIN/state.
- Approvals and payment rejections both let the salesperson fix & resubmit the same order.
