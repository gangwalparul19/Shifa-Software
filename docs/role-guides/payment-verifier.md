# Payment Verifier — Quick-Start Guide

As a **Payment Verifier** you confirm that customer payments are genuine before an order is fulfilled.

## Where you land
After sign-in you go straight to **Payments** (your work queue). Your mobile bottom tabs are
**Payments · Orders · My Profile**.

## Your queue
Payments shows prepaid orders awaiting verification, **newest first**, with a count tile.

## Verify a payment
1. Open an order from the queue.
2. Review the **payment screenshot(s)** in the tabbed "snip" viewer against the **expected amount**
   (and the salesperson shown).
3. Watch for the red **Duplicate proof** badge — it warns if the same screenshot image was already
   used on another order.
4. Choose:
   - **Verify** — the payment is genuine; the order proceeds.
   - **Reject** — marks the order **Payment Rejected** (with a reason). The salesperson can then fix
     and resubmit it.

## What you can also see
- **Orders:** the all-orders view (read-only context).
- **My Profile:** request changes to your own details (an admin approves them).

## Golden rules
- Match the **amount** on the screenshot to the order's expected amount.
- Flag anything suspicious — reject with a clear reason so the salesperson knows what to fix.
- You don't approve or dispatch orders — only the payment decision is yours.
