# Packing User — Quick-Start Guide

As a **Packing User** you prepare parcels: print labels, pack, hand over, and dispatch. This is the
warehouse floor screen.

## Where you land
After sign-in you go straight to **Packing**. Your 4 mobile bottom tabs are **Packing · Pick-list ·
Mark RTO · Orders**.

## The Packing page
At the top: KPI tiles (awaiting pack / handover / dispatch), a **scan box**, and a **camera** button.

### Scan & Move (fastest way)
Scan an order's barcode (keyboard scanner or phone camera). The app shows the order, customer, status,
and any **packaging note**, then asks you to confirm the next step: **Pack → Handover → Dispatch**.
The barcode works whether it encodes the QuikShip order id or the order code.

### Work queues (each shows order, customer, price, date, salesperson, and the note)
1. **Orders to Pack:** print the internal label and/or the **QuikShip label**, then **Mark packed**.
   Printing the QuikShip label moves the order to Awaiting Handover automatically. Select several and
   **print labels in bulk** (they print 4-up on A4 — cut into quarters).
2. **Awaiting Handover:** record **who took the parcel** (handover name) — one at a time or in bulk.
   For a courier order this makes it Ready for Pickup.
3. **QuickShip Status** (read-only): the courier's AWB + status, updated automatically.
4. **In-House Deliveries:** for parcels your own team delivers — select orders and **update the status
   manually** (Out for Delivery, Delivered, …).

## Other tools
- **Pick-list** (bottom tab): one sheet listing every product needed across all orders waiting to be
  packed — pick everything in one trip.
- **Mark RTO** (bottom tab): scan a parcel that came back and mark it **Returned to Origin** with a
  reason.
- **Package count:** set how many boxes an order ships in — it prints that many label copies.

## Reading the note
Each queue row and the scan preview show the **packaging note** (amber flag) — always check it for
special instructions before you pack.

## Golden rules
- Always **print the label** and **mark packed** so the order advances and appears to the courier.
- Capture the **handover name** so there's a record of who took each parcel.
- For in-house parcels, remember to **update the status** — no courier will do it for you.
