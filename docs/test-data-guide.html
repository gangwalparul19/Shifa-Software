What’s in V27

All rows use IDs that cannot collide with V22 or with runtime-generated data (users 101–123, orders 1000–1119 with codes SHR-5001–SHR-5120, courier companies 2–4, suppliers 10–14, purchase orders 10–14). Every timestamp is relative to NOW(), so the data always looks like “the last six months” whenever it is applied.

Navigation guide by role

The admin app is mobile-first: a persistent bottom tab bar gives each role its 4 most-used screens, while the hamburger menu (top-left) opens the full role menu, including the Shifa dashboard.

Admin full oversight

  • Shifa Dashboard — KPI tiles, fulfilment queues, insights summary.
  • Approval Queue — 8 orders sit in PENDING_ADMIN_APPROVAL; approve/reject them.
  • Insights — click Recompute to generate insights (low-stock, RTO risk, courier scorecard, COD build-up, lead-source conversion), then review by severity.
  • Reports — orders by lead source / status / salesperson, delivery outcomes.
  • Finance / P&L — monthly revenue vs. expenses (cost signal every month).
  • Suppliers / Purchase Orders — POs across DRAFT → RECEIVED → CANCELLED.

Salesperson sell & follow up

  • New Order — capture a customer order (pick lead source).
  • Orders — your orders across every lifecycle stage.
  • Leads — pipeline filter tabs (NEW/CONTACTED/QUOTED/WON/LOST); capture, advance, mark lost, convert.
  • Due follow-ups — leads due today and overdue are flagged (reminder job feeds the bell).
  • My dashboard widgets — lead pipeline counts + due-follow-up badge.

Packing user fulfilment

  • Packing — orders at LABEL_GENERATED ready to pack.
  • Handover — move PACKED → HANDED_TO_DELIVERY.
  • Dispatch — hand parcels to couriers; watch them flow to COURIER_ASSIGNED / DISPATCHED.

Accountant money in

  • Reconciliation — COD receivables; some settled, some still outstanding for signal.
  • Reports — revenue & delivery-outcome breakdowns.
  • Expenses — 21 expenses spread monthly across RENT / SALARIES / MARKETING / PACKAGING / UTILITIES / LOGISTICS.

How to apply

Locally: just restart the backend — Flyway runs on startup and applies V27 automatically.
On the server: it auto-applies on the next deploy (Flyway runs before the app serves traffic).

V27 is additive and uses non-colliding IDs, so it will not disturb existing data (including the V22 demo dataset) and it is safe to ship. It seeds no schema changes — data only. Because all dates are NOW()-relative, the dataset never “ages out”; it always represents the most recent ~6 months.
