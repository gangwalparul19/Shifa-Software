Shifa OMS — User Manual

The complete guide to the Shifa Herbal Remedies Order Management System. Use the role switcher on the left to focus the manual on your job, or search any term.

Order managementCRM & leadsPacking & delivery Payments & GSTAnalyticsMobile-first PWA

Shifa OMS is a dashboard-only order management system. The public store runs on Shopify; those orders flow in automatically. Staff use this system to approve, pack, deliver, verify payments, reconcile money, handle GST, and track performance.

About screenshots This manual describes each screen rather than embedding images, because the production system holds real customer data. For captures, use a local development instance.

1 · Getting started

Shifa OMS is a web app you open in a browser. It is mobile-first and works on a phone and on a laptop/desktop.

  • On a phone/tablet: a top bar with a menu button (☰) and a bar of four main tabs along the bottom for your most-used tasks.
  • On a laptop/desktop (screen ≥ ~992px): a fixed menu down the left side instead of the bottom bar.

What you see depends on your role, assigned when your account is created.

2 · Signing in & your account

Signing in

  1. Open the application URL in your browser.
  2. Enter your username and password, then select Sign in. Salespeople usually sign in with their mobile number as the username.
  3. You land on your role's home screen (see the table below).

First login / password reset

  • If an admin reset your password, you'll be sent to a Change password screen and must set a new one before continuing.
  • You can change your own password anytime from the account menu.

Your session

  • You stay signed in while you work; the app silently refreshes an expiring session. If it can't, you're returned to sign-in.
  • All dates and times are shown in India Standard Time (IST).

My Profile (everyone)

Open the user chip → My Profile. You can submit changes to your details, but they're not applied immediately — they go to an admin as a change request. Photo and government-ID changes are handled by an admin.

3 · Finding your way around

The menu

  • Mobile: tap ☰ to open the grouped drawer (Orders, CRM, Catalog, …).
  • Desktop: the same groups appear as a permanent left sidebar; the current group opens automatically.

You only see menu items your role is allowed to use.

Bottom tabs (mobile)

Four tabs for your role's most common tasks — see your role quick-start for the exact four.

Global search / command palette Press Ctrl+K (or Cmd+K) anywhere to find orders, customers, products, and jump to quick actions for your role.

Page conventions

  • Lists are cards on a phone, tables on a desktop, and always paginated.
  • Detail drawers slide in from the right and use tabs to avoid long scrolling.
  • Status pills are colour-coded: done waiting problem in progress

Scoping A salesperson sees only their own orders/customers/leads. A team lead sees their assigned team's orders (plus their own). Admin / Accountant / CA are unscoped. CA is read-only.

5 · Role quick-starts

A short, task-focused start for each role. Pick your role in the left switcher to hide the others.

Admin

Home: DashboardTabs: Approvals · Orders · Products · Reports

You run the whole business. Your daily flow:

  1. Approve new orders — Orders → Approval Queue. Review details + payment screenshots, then Approve or Reject with a reason. Use bulk approve with Quick-select-by-date; a preview shows how many will be skipped.
  2. Watch the Exception Center — approvals waiting, payment issues, failed deliveries, claims, top insights, each with a direct action.
  3. Keep fulfilment moving — Packing, QuikShip/Shopify sync.

Only you: Settings (company + GST + auto-approval + Shopify switch), Users, Profile approvals, Teams & Team-wise Sales, Cancel Order, Deleted orders, Shopify Sync, Audit Log, Backups, Announcements. You can also punch orders on behalf of a salesperson/team lead.

Golden rule Use RTO (not Cancel) to give up on a post-invoice/delivered order — Cancel would drop a filed invoice out of GST.

Salesperson

Home: Dashboard (My day)Tabs: New Order · Orders · Customers · Products

You take orders and manage leads & your own customers (you see only your own).

Take a new order (your main job)

  1. Customer: enter the mobile; accept the pre-fill if they've ordered before. Pick the destination (India → pincode auto-fills; or Outside India) and lead source.
  2. Items: add products (Quick-add best-sellers / frequently-bought-together); rates stay within each product's min–MRP band.
  3. Payment: collect at least ₹100 upfront (full or partial), and upload the payment screenshot(s) — required when any amount is received.
  4. Review: check totals/GST, add a note/discount, Save → Pending Approval.

If an order is rejected, open it, read the reason, and Fix & resubmit (same order, history kept). Manage Leads (New→Contacted→Quoted→Won/Lost, follow-ups, Convert) and Customers (360, Reorder, Call/WhatsApp).

Golden rule Always collect ₹100+ upfront and attach the screenshot, or the order won't save.

Team Lead

Home: DashboardTabs: Dashboard · Orders · Performance · My Profile

You oversee your assigned salespeople (read-only on orders) and can punch orders for the team.

  • Your team's orders — list, search, open, download invoices.
  • Team Performance — period controls, team KPIs, a salesperson leaderboard (click a person to drill into their 360), lead-source conversion, and a My orders / Team orders split.
  • New Order — orders you punch are attributed to you and scoped to your team.
  • Manage shared WhatsApp templates.
If you see only your own orders Ask an admin to assign your salespeople to you on the Teams page.

Packing User

Home: PackingTabs: Packing · Pick-list · Mark RTO · Orders

You prepare parcels. Every approved order flows through packing.

Scan & Move (fastest)

Scan an order's barcode (scanner or phone camera); confirm the next step Pack → Handover → Dispatch. Check the amber packaging note.

Work queues

  1. Orders to Pack: print the internal/QuikShip label (bulk prints 4-up on A4), then Mark packed. Printing the QuikShip label advances to Awaiting Handover.
  2. Awaiting Handover: record who took the parcel (single or bulk). For courier orders this makes it Ready for Pickup.
  3. QuickShip Status (read-only): AWB + status, updated automatically.
  4. In-House Deliveries: select orders and update status manually.

Pick-list: everything to pick for all pending orders on one sheet. Mark RTO: scan a returned parcel & mark Returned to Origin with a reason.

Payment Verifier

Home: PaymentsTabs: Payments · Orders · My Profile

You confirm payments are genuine before fulfilment.

  1. Open an order from the queue (newest first).
  2. Review the screenshot(s) in the tabbed "snip" viewer against the expected amount.
  3. Watch for the red Duplicate proof badge (same image used elsewhere).
  4. Verify (genuine → proceeds) or Reject (marks the order Payment Rejected; the salesperson can fix & resubmit).
Golden rule Match the amount on the screenshot to the order; reject with a clear reason. You don't approve or dispatch — only the payment decision is yours.

Accountant

Home: DashboardTabs: Reconcile · Reports · Expenses · Orders

You track the money. Main screen: Reconciliation.

  • Receivables / Unsettled COD / Pending claims — oldest first.
  • COD aging / SLA — buckets by age; anything over SLA is flagged.
  • Courier remittance import — upload the QuikShipX CSV/Excel; it matches & settles rows (and marks missed deliveries Delivered + COD Collected). Preview first.

You can approve orders. Record Expenses, review P&L, and work the full general ledger (Voucher Entry/post, Day Book, Trial Balance, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow). Reports include money & receivables + operations.

Golden rule Always preview a remittance import before committing. Chase the oldest receivables / over-SLA COD first.

CA (Chartered Accountant)

Home: GST & AccountingTabs: GST · Reports · Finance · My Profile

You handle GST & accounting. Read-only on operations.

  • GST & Accounting dashboard: money in/out, GSTR-3B summary with manual ITC + net payable, rate/HSN/state breakup, an Export segment (outside-India @ 18% IGST), period presets (incl. Indian FY/quarters), row drill-down, CSV/PDF export.
  • GST Filing: period calendar, prepare/file/reopen, snapshots, filing-aware export.
  • GST Reconciliation: five compared figures with drill-down.
  • Books (read-only): Expenses, P&L, Reconciliation, Returns, and the full ledger (Chart of Accounts, Day Book, Trial Balance, statements). Posting is Admin/Accountant only.
Golden rule Reconcile before you file. RTO reverses a sale with a credit note; a post-invoice order should be RTO'd, never cancelled. ITC is entered manually.

6 · Dashboard

Your dashboard is shaped by your role.

  • Admin: KPIs, a clickable "needs attention" strip (approvals/exceptions/ handover), a channel overview (Portal vs Shopify vs All), team-wise sales, live activity.
  • Salesperson: "My day" (today's orders/revenue, monthly target, to-collect), a task inbox (reorder-due + win-back), and your lead pipeline.
  • Team Lead: team-scoped status, team highlights, link to Team Performance.
  • Accountant: money/receivables KPIs linking into Reconciliation.
  • Payment verifier / CA / Packing: taken straight to their work screen.

7 · Orders

Orders → Orders. Every staff role can open this (scoped by role).

Finding orders

  • Status tabs group orders into business stages: Pending Approval, Processing, Shipped, Delivered, Returned/Failed, Cancelled (and Rejected).
  • Search by order code, name, or mobile (jumps the tab to "All").
  • Advanced filters: status group, payment status, source, date, (admin) a salesperson. Save a combination as a saved view.

Reading an order

Click a row for the detail drawer with tabs: Details (customer, address, fulfilment, shipment/AWB + Track link, invoice download), Items (lines, subtotal/discount/GST/total), Payment (status, amount, verification, screenshots). The timeline and (for admins) raw status history are shown too.

Actions (role-dependent)

  • Download invoice · Print label (admin/packing) · Edit (admin: PENDING/APPROVED; creator: own PENDING) · Approve/Reject (admin/accountant) · Fix & resubmit · Reorder · Export list to Excel/CSV.

8 · Taking a new order

SalespersonAdminTeam Lead

Orders → New Order — guided, or a single-screen Quick mode.

  1. Customer: mobile (with pre-fill), destination (India/Outside India), lead source (Counter Sale = in-house). Admins can place it on behalf of someone.
  2. Items: add products; rates within the min–MRP band; SKU/barcode quick-add.
  3. Payment: ₹100+ upfront (full or partial), upload screenshot(s).
  4. Review: totals/GST, optional note & discount, Save → Pending Approval.
Duplicate guard You can't create a second same-day order for the same product for the same customer (different items on the same day are fine). Unfinished orders are saved as a draft to resume.

9 · Approving orders

AdminAccountant

Orders → Approval Queue. Review details + screenshots; Approve (into fulfilment) or Reject with a reason category + note. Bulk approve with Quick-select-by-date; a preview shows skipped ones. New orders raise a live toast + chime.

10 · Payment verification

Payment VerifierAdmin

Orders → Payments. Open an order, review the screenshot "snips" vs the expected amount, watch for the Duplicate proof badge, then Verify or Reject (marks the order Payment Rejected; salesperson can resubmit).

11 · Packing & fulfilment

Packing UserAdmin

Orders → Packing. Scan box + camera; KPI tiles; four queues:

  1. Orders to Pack: print label(s) → Mark packed (bulk prints 4-up on A4).
  2. Awaiting Handover: capture who took the parcel.
  3. QuickShip Status (read-only, auto-updated).
  4. In-House Deliveries: update status manually.

Also: Pick-list (one aggregated sheet), Mark RTO, and package count (prints that many label copies).

12 · Delivery & tracking

  • QuikShipX (courier): created on punch, AWB on approval, auto-tracked. The drawer shows status, AWB, and a Track link. Admins can Retry tracking ID.
  • In-house ("Ishika Enterprise"): your team delivers; staff update status manually and settle COD on delivery.
  • Customers can be sent a public tracking link (no login).
  • Shopify Sync (admin): recover stuck Shopify orders; on/off switch.

13 · Returns, RTO & cancellations

  • Returns (admin/accountant/CA): create/approve/reject returns & refunds.
  • RTO: the GST-correct "give up" — reverses the sale with a credit note.
  • Retry delivery: re-attempt a failed/refused delivery.
  • Cancel Order (admin): cancel with a note, even after an AWB (aborts courier pickup).
Do not cancel a post-invoice order It would drop an already-filed invoice from GST. Use RTO instead.

24 · WhatsApp messaging

Order and customer screens have Call & WhatsApp buttons. WhatsApp uses click-to-chat with customizable templates — placeholders like {name}, {orderCode}, {total}, {remaining}, {orderSummary} are filled in automatically. Managers maintain templates under WhatsApp templates.

25 · Mobile app (PWA) & offline

  • Install the app to your home screen/desktop from the top bar.
  • An Offline chip appears when you lose connection. Creating orders needs connectivity (₹100 + screenshot), so new orders can't be queued offline.
  • An update banner appears on a new version — reload to get it; hard-refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R) if a screen looks out of date.

26 · Order lifecycle (reference)

An order moves through these business stages:

Pending Approval→ Processing (approved, label, packed, handover)→ Shipped (courier/in-house)→ Delivered→ Closed / COD Collected
Out for Delivery→ Customer Rejected / Delivery Failed→ RetryorRTO (credit note)
  • Rejected (admin) and Payment Rejected (verifier) can be Fixed & resubmitted — same order, history kept.
  • Cancelled is for pre-delivery orders only; use RTO after invoicing.
  • REDISPATCH = parcel lost/damaged by courier (raises a claim, clears dues).

28 · Troubleshooting

"Forbidden" / can't open a pageYour role doesn't have access. The menu only shows what you can use.
Returned to the sign-in screenYour session expired. Sign in again.
"This record changed elsewhere" (409)Someone else updated it. Refresh and retry.
New order won't save — screenshot requiredAttach the payment screenshot in the Payment step.
New order blocked as a duplicateThe customer already has a same-day order for that product. Different items are allowed.
Order stuck without a tracking idAdmin: use Retry tracking ID, or Shopify Sync for Shopify orders.
WhatsApp emoji show as "□"Use simple symbols in templates; some emoji don't survive WhatsApp Desktop.
