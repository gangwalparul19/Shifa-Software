🚀 How to use it

Every staff member logs in with their own username and lands on a screen shaped for their job. Here is the day-to-day flow.

Getting started

Open the app on any device

It's a mobile-first web app (PWA) — works in the browser on a phone, tablet or desktop. It can be "installed" to the home screen like an app.

Log in with your role

Each person has their own login. The menu, bottom tabs and available actions change automatically based on whether you are Admin, Salesperson, Packing, Accountant, etc.

Do your part of the flow

Salesperson punches orders, Admin approves, Packing packs & dispatches, everyone sees the live status. The system pushes it forward and notifies the right people.

A real order, start to finish

Enquiry & payment

Customer enquires on WhatsApp/Instagram. Team shares a payment QR and the customer sends a payment screenshot.

Salesperson punches the order

Customer name, mobile, address; products with editable rates; amount received + the payment screenshot upload. The system auto-calculates COD/remaining and sets payment status (Fully Paid / Partially Paid / COD).

Admin reviews & approves

Admin checks the screenshot, items, rates and totals in the Approval Queue, then approves (or rejects with a reason).

Label prints automatically

On approval the system generates an internal packing label (Order ID + barcode + COD amount + customer & items) as a PDF, ready to print.

Packing packs & scans

Packing sees a live work queue, prints the label, scans the barcode to mark "Packed", then hands over / dispatches. Courier & AWB get attached.

Customer gets WhatsApp updates

Dispatched, out for delivery, delivered — the customer is messaged with tracking link and COD amount where relevant.

Money is reconciled

Prepaid orders close on delivery; COD becomes "collect from courier"; RTO auto-cancels COD; lost shipments become a claim on the courier. All visible in the reconciliation dashboard.

🔄 Order status flow

Every order moves through a controlled sequence — no status can be skipped or reversed illegally. Tap any status to see what happens there.

Pending Approval→ Approved→ Label Generated→ Packed→ Handed to Delivery→ Courier Assigned→ Dispatched→ In Transit→ Out for Delivery→ Delivered
Outcomes & exceptions: COD Collected / Closed RTO Redispatch Rejected / Failed Rejected / Cancelled

Tap a status above

Each stage authorises the right role, checks the move is legal, records one history row, writes an audit event and fans out the right notifications.

Behind the scenes: a single workflow engine enforces the rules — it checks who is allowed to make the move, whether the move is legal from the current status, then updates status, writes history, records an audit event and sends notifications. That is why the data stays clean and trustworthy.

✨ Features

The platform is organised into modules. Tap a heading to expand it.

  • Salesperson order entry: customer details, product picker with editable per-line rates, quantity.
  • Auto-calculated totals, amount received, remaining/COD, and payment status (Fully Paid / Partially Paid / COD).
  • Mandatory payment screenshot upload when money is received — stored durably against the order.
  • Optional order note, lead source (WhatsApp / Instagram / Facebook / Google / Offline), and customer email.
  • Admin approval queue with approve / reject-with-reason.
  • Automatic internal label PDF on approval: Order ID + Code128 barcode + customer, address, items and COD amount.
  • Single & bulk label printing / re-print.
  • Packing work queues: to-pack, awaiting handover, awaiting dispatch — oldest first.
  • Barcode scan to confirm "Packed"; handover & dispatch actions.
  • Courier shipping label + AWB with remaining COD amount.
  • Courier assignment + tracking; live status updates (In Transit → Out for Delivery → Delivered / RTO / Lost).
  • Customer WhatsApp messages at each key step: courier name, tracking link, COD amount, estimated delivery.
  • Email notifications on Approved / Dispatched / Delivered; in-app alerts to the right roles.
  • Single notification engine so messages are consistent and never duplicated.
  • Ready to go live Courier & WhatsApp Business APIs are wired behind swappable interfaces (currently simulated).
  • Prepaid orders auto-close on delivery with nil outstanding.
  • COD becomes "collect from courier"; settlement date tracking per courier.
  • RTO auto-cancels COD; lost/damaged shipments become a claim on the courier for the full amount.
  • Separate reports: delivered-COD to collect, loss claims to file, prepaid vs COD segregation.
  • Product catalog with SKU, MRP, sale price, GST rate, images, CSV import.
  • Stock levels with movements (restock / sale / return / adjustment) and low-stock alerts.
  • Suppliers & purchase orders; receiving feeds inventory restock.
  • Per-product current-month sales & order stats.
  • Lead capture with source & status pipeline (New → Contacted → Quoted → Won/Lost).
  • Follow-up reminders (with overdue flags) pushed to the owner.
  • One-click convert lead → order (prefills the new-order form).
  • Customer list derived from orders, scoped per salesperson.
  • Lead reports: by source, conversion, pipeline, lost reasons.
  • GST tax invoices (CGST/SGST/IGST + HSN) and plain invoices, with invoice numbering series.
  • Expenses & Profit-and-Loss view (revenue vs costs vs claims).
  • Returns / refunds / RTO workflow.
  • Company & GST settings, bank details, T&C, logo, delivery-state master list.
  • Global search by customer / mobile / order ID / AWB, with repeat-order detection.
  • Persistent notifications center + per-user bell (read/unread).
  • Global, searchable audit log — who changed what, when.
  • Nightly database backups.
  • Complete staff records — not just a name: full name, mobile, email, address, joining date and role.
  • ID / document verification before onboarding — upload proof (Aadhaar / PAN / photo) so the admin has a verified record for every salesperson before they can be activated.
  • Verification status (pending / verified / rejected) so no one goes live without approved proof.
  • Admin "All salespeople" directory — one screen listing every salesperson with their details, documents, status and activity.
  • Activate / deactivate staff, and tie every order and action back to a verified, on-record person.

🛡️ Technology & security

Built like real business software, not a template site.

Built on

  • Backend: Java 21 / Spring Boot (modular monolith), MySQL 8 database.
  • Frontend: Angular — mobile-first, installable PWA, clean Shifa-green theme with charts.
  • Documents: server-generated PDF invoices & labels, real barcodes.
  • Cloud hosting with a reverse proxy, HTTPS and a systemd service.

Trust & safety

  • Secure login with role-based access control (JWT).
  • Every change is authorised, validated and audited.
  • Database schema is version-controlled (Flyway migrations).
  • 490+ automated tests guard the core logic against regressions.
  • Automatic nightly backups; durable storage for payment screenshots.
Why it's more reliable: the money math, the status rules and the permissions are all covered by automated tests that run on every change — so new features don't quietly break what already works.
