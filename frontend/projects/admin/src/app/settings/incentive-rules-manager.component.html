Daily sales incentive

A salesperson earns a flat bonus for each day their sales reach a slab. A day earns the highest slab it reaches (slabs do not add up), and a day that exactly meets a slab qualifies. Sales are the order totals punched that day, without rejected, payment-rejected or cancelled orders. Every save is added to the history below; earlier rules are never overwritten, and each past day keeps the rules that applied on it.
