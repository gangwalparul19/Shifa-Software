Data downloads for analysts

Flat CSV extracts of your sales data. Column names are fixed and written in snake_case, dates are ISO (yyyy-mm-dd), and numbers are raw (no currency symbols or thousands separators).

Privacy. Orders, order items and daily sales carry no mobile numbers. A one-way customer_key joins the tables, so you can still count repeat buyers. Only the Customers dataset has names and mobile numbers, for campaigns.

Ways to use them
  • Open a file in a spreadsheet, or load it into Power BI, Metabase or a notebook.
  • Join the tables on order_id and customer_key.
  • Download every week to keep your own history.
Good to know
  • Every download is recorded in the audit log.
  • The period you chose on this page applies to datasets that use a date range. Customers is always all time.
  • Orders, order items and daily sales hold up to 100,000 rows and at most 366 days. If a download is refused, choose a shorter period.
